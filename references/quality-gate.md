# Quality Gate

Run this check before delivering a Mooselog travel journal.

## Product Fit

- Does the result read as a travel journal page, not just a route map, dashboard, poster, or photo collage?
- Is the page designed as a complete editorial notebook spread?
- Are route, photos, notes, and checklists integrated into one visual system?

## Structure

- Is there a clear title/day/date area?
- Is there a schedule, route order, or phase list?
- Is there a map/route field or symbolic journey field?
- Are there practical support modules such as weather, knowledge, goals, food, transport, reservations, tips, or checklist?
- Are modules varied in size and hierarchy instead of equal boxes?
- When a strict split layout was requested, are all information modules on the left and the complete illustrated map on the right?

## Route Logic

- Does every stop or phase appear exactly once unless repeats are intentional?
- Do map pins match schedule numbers?
- Are arrows, dotted routes, or line direction unambiguous?
- Is exact geography only claimed when coordinates or reliable map references support it?

## Photo Use

- Are photos used as evidence, stickers, sketches, or landmark references rather than a dominant photo wall?
- Does each photo crop preserve the useful subject?
- If photos were stylized or illustrated, does the place identity remain recognizable?
- In complex-hand-drawn mode, are all landmarks genuinely illustrated rather than realistic photos inside hand-drawn frames?

## Design Quality

- Is there a strong visual entry point?
- Does the page have design rhythm: large area, medium cards, small tags, and detail accents?
- Are icons and stickers tied to actual content?
- Are colors assigned consistent jobs?
- Does the page feel save-worthy and authored?
- Are left-side card fills and borders sampled from the map palette rather than from an unrelated set of colors?
- Are map labels compact, paper-toned, and limited to place names when that mode was requested?
- When controlled asymmetry was requested, do support cards vary in position/height without making the page look accidental or reducing readability?
- Is the page ground derived from the destination rather than default sand or vintage beige?
- Would the palette still suggest the destination if the title were hidden?

## Domestic China Differentiation

For `domestic-china` outputs:

- Does the page use a Chinese composition or map grammar rather than the international template with the flag removed?
- Are Chinese elements structural, region-specific, and supported by the destination?
- Are at least three destination-specific Chinese visual signals present?
- Is the result contemporary and useful rather than generic antique styling?
- Are culturally sensitive, religious, ethnic, official, and regional motifs verified and used respectfully?

## Social-Media Readability

- Does the image work at the requested ratio?
- Is Chinese text readable on a phone?
- Can the user understand the route/order without reading every paragraph?
- Are warnings, reservations, and map links visible enough?
- Does every text block sit in a dedicated quiet/opaque safe zone with no route, photo, landmark, flag, or decoration crossing it?
- Are long place names wrapped or resized without touching neighboring content?

## Typography And Licensing

- Does every Chinese and Latin glyph use a font bundled in `assets/fonts/` with an accompanying commercial-use-compatible license?
- Is the license file present and is the output free of fallback glyph boxes?
- Do not claim legal certainty beyond the included license; preserve the license file whenever redistributing the font assets.

## International Pages

- Is the destination country identifiable through a subtle flag watermark or equivalent marker?
- Is the flag low-opacity, placed in the upper-right map area, and kept behind primary labels?

## Author Credit

- Was the user asked whether to include an author credit?
- If included, does the credit exactly match the supplied display name and sit quietly in the lower-right area?
- If declined, is the image free of inferred author marks?

## Honesty

- Do not invent weather, prices, coordinates, opening hours, road status, or official booking rules.
- Mark uncertain time-sensitive items with "出发前确认".
- Do not add watermarks unless requested.
- Keep skill authorship in the skill itself: Mooselog Travel Journal by Mooselog.
